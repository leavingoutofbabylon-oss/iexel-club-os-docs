## [1.0.0] — Pending

### Added
- Complete People management (CRUD, roles, WordPress user linking, club ID generation)
- Team management (CRUD, assignments, age groups, football formats)
- Season management (lifecycle, team seasons, reconciliation, backfill)
- Season Planning (plan generation, approval, transactional execution)
- Venue management (CRUD, map link formatting)
- Event management (lifecycle, audience, team workspace)
- Attendance management (availability, attendance register)
- Match Mode (lineup, state, score, goals, substitutions, goalkeeper changes, incidents, undo)
- Formation Engine (system templates for 5v5, 7v7, 9v9, 11v11)
- Football Statistics (player and team statistics, eligibility policy)
- Player Registrations (administrator and family registration workflows)
- Finance management (invoices, payments, billing schedules, fee rules, discount policies, billing runs)
- Communications (email sending, scheduling, templates, delivery audit)
- Member Portal (five experience roles: Parent, Player, Coach, Committee, Treasurer)
- Executive Dashboard (widgets, alerts, 60-second cache)
- Club Projects (full project lifecycle, visibility, ordering)
- Branding and Media (brand palette, login branding, app icon, entity media)
- Activity Log (global activity log)
- 26 custom WordPress capabilities
- 6 custom WordPress roles
- 45 admin pages
- 19 portal routes
- Controlled database upgrade pipeline (UpgradeRunner)
- Release Readiness check page
